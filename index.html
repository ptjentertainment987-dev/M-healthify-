GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are meals customisable?

Not at the moment. Meals follow the published weekly menu and fixed plan portions. Please subscribe only if the listed menu and plan structure work for you.

Can I get a refund after paying?

All subscription sales are final and payments are non-refundable once an order is confirmed. Please review your plan, duration, menu and delivery details carefully before paying. If M Healthify cannot fulfil an order, contact us to discuss an appropriate resolution.

How do I know my order is confirmed?

Your order is confirmed only after payment verification and a confirmation from M Healthify. Submitting the form is a request, not a confirmed booking.

Can the calorie and protein values vary?

Small variations may occur due to ingredient brands, natural food variation and preparation. We aim to keep portions aligned with the published plan targets.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery availability and charges (if any) are confirmed by our team based on your full address before your subscription begins.

How are my daily meals delivered?

All 3 meals included in your daily subscription are delivered together in one single morning delivery, between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM. We do not make separate breakfast, lunch and dinner deliveries.
